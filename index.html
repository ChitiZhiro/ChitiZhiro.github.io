Business Problem

Analyzed video game sales data using Microsoft Excel to organize records, retrieve information, and summarize sales performance. Used PivotTables, PivotCharts, and interactive slicers to create a structured and easy-to-explore sales report.

Objectives
  • Organize and structure raw video game sales records using Excel Tables
  • Retrieve specific game information using XLOOKUP and Filters
  • Summarize sales performance using PivotTables
  • Create interactive PivotCharts with slicers for data exploration
  • Combine data columns using HSTACK and SUM for reporting views
Dataset

Video game sales dataset (vgsales.csv) sourced from Kaggle — 16,598 records covering titles released from 1980 to 2016, with columns: Rank, Name, Platform, Year, Genre, Publisher, NA_Sales, EU_Sales, JP_Sales, Other_Sales, Global_Sales (in millions of units).

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel — XLOOKUP, Filters, Excel Tables, SUM, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers.

Key Techniques
  • Converted raw data to structured Excel Table for dynamic references and filtering
  • XLOOKUP for retrieving game-specific sales records by title
  • Filters applied to explore data by platform, genre, and region
  • PivotTables to summarize total sales by platform, genre, and year
  • Slicer-driven PivotCharts for interactive data exploration
Key Findings

PS2 dominated global sales as the top platform. Action and Sports genres led across all regions. North America was the largest market, followed by Europe. Nintendo titles showed strong longevity across multiple platforms. The dataset revealed a clear decline in physical game sales post-2010, aligning with the rise of digital distribution.

Project Overview

This project focuses on e-commerce sales analysis using MySQL to extract business insights from customer, order, and product data. Developed SQL queries to analyze monthly revenue, average order value, payment methods, customer purchasing behavior, repeat-customer rate, month-over-month revenue growth, and product performance. Applied advanced SQL techniques to identify sales trends, track cumulative revenue, rank products, and evaluate customer spending patterns.

Business Objectives
  • Analyze monthly revenue and month-over-month sales growth.
  • Calculate average order value and revenue by payment method.
  • Identify customers with no orders and measure repeat-customer rate.
  • Track daily revenue and cumulative sales performance.
  • Rank top-performing products within each category and identify customers' latest orders.
SQL Skills Demonstrated

MySQL — SUM, COUNT, AVG, ROUND, GROUP BY, HAVING, WHERE, ORDER BY, DATE_FORMAT, DATE, INNER JOIN, LEFT JOIN, Subqueries, CTEs, LAG, ROW_NUMBER, DENSE_RANK, SUM() OVER (Window Functions).

Monthly revenue and month-over-month growth (Window Function) LAG + DATE_FORMAT 
WITH monthly AS (
  SELECT
    DATE_FORMAT(order_date, '%Y-%m') AS month,
    SUM(total_amount) AS revenue
  FROM orders
  GROUP BY month
)
SELECT
  month, revenue,
  LAG(revenue) OVER (ORDER BY month) AS prev_month,
  ROUND((revenue - LAG(revenue) OVER (ORDER BY month))
    / LAG(revenue) OVER (ORDER BY month) * 100, 2) AS mom_growth_pct
FROM monthly ORDER BY month;
Average order value and revenue by payment method GROUP BY + AVG 
SELECT
  payment_method,
  COUNT(order_id) AS total_orders,
  SUM(total_amount) AS total_revenue,
  ROUND(AVG(total_amount), 2) AS avg_order_value
FROM orders
GROUP BY payment_method
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;

-- Overall AOV
SELECT
  ROUND(AVG(total_amount), 2) AS overall_aov
FROM orders;
Repeat-customer rate and customers with no orders LEFT JOIN + Subquery 
SELECT
    c.name,
    SUM(o.total_amount) AS total_spent
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
GROUP BY c.customer_id, c.name
HAVING total_spent > (
    SELECT AVG(total_amount) FROM orders
)
ORDER BY total_spent DESC;
SQL Skills: Aggregations (SUM, COUNT, AVG, ROUND) · Grouping & Filtering (GROUP BY, HAVING, WHERE, ORDER BY) · Date Functions (DATE_FORMAT, DATE) · Joins (INNER JOIN, LEFT JOIN) · Subqueries & CTEs · Window Functions (LAG, ROW_NUMBER, DENSE_RANK, SUM() OVER) · Business Metrics: Monthly Revenue, AOV, Repeat-Customer Rate, MoM Growth, Cumulative Revenue.
Key Findings & Insights
Top Customers

A small group of repeat customers accounted for a disproportionately large share of total revenue — a classic 80/20 pattern surfaced through JOIN + GROUP BY aggregation.

Category Revenue

Electronics and Clothing emerged as the top revenue-generating categories. HAVING filtered out low-volume categories, keeping the analysis focused on meaningful segments.

Payment Trends

Credit card was the dominant payment method, followed by UPI and net banking. This insight directly informs checkout optimization decisions for e-commerce teams.

High-Value Customers

The subquery approach identified customers spending above the average order value — a reusable pattern applicable to CRM segmentation and targeted sales outreach.
